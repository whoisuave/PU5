    Ace! Pick the new suit

    All Saints Rules

    Be the first to run out of cards. Each turn, play one card or a run of cards.

    • Your first card must match the suit or number/letter on the pile.
    • To play more than one card, each card must be the same number or go up or down by one from the last. Suits don't matter (5♦ → 5♠ → 5♣ → 6♣, or 4 → 5 → 4).
    • Can't (or won't) play? Pick up a card and lose your turn.
    • A: play it any time (even at the end of a run) and pick the new suit.
    • 2: next player picks up 2.
    • 7: everyone passes their hand anticlockwise.
    • 8: skip the next player.
    • Black J: pick up 5 (with 2 decks: your starting hand size).
    • Red J: cancels a pick-up that's in play. It must still match the pile.
    • Q: only goes on her suit or another Queen, and must be covered by her suit or another Queen. Can't cover? Pick up a card.
    • K: reverses direction (starts clockwise).
    • In play: after a 2 or black Jack, the deck is hot. Stack another 2 / black Jack (the total keeps growing), cancel with a red Jack, or pick up the lot. Your run must end on a 2, black Jack or red Jack.
    • All Saints II mode: you can also cancel with a combo. Start with any pick-up power card and carry on in order, e.g. (red J, 10), (2, 3) or (black J, Q, cover).
    • No power card can start or finish the game. You can't go out on A, 2, 7, 8, J, Q or K. The first card played after the deal can be a power card (on 3♣ you can play Q♣ then 4♣).
    • The dealer can't start on a power card, the player left of the dealer goes first, and the dealer reshuffles the play pile (keeping the top card) when the pickup deck runs out.

    How to play here: tap cards in the order you want to play them, then press Play. Tap a staged card to put it (and the cards after it) back.
